## What is student attendance software?

**Student attendance software** replaces paper registers with a digital system for recording who is present, late or absent each day. Good attendance software is quick for teachers, accurate for the office, and transparent for parents.

In Evolvora Campus, a teacher opens their class, taps **Present, Late or Absent** for each student (or marks everyone present and flags the exceptions), and totals update live. Attendance feeds straight into dashboards and the parent app, so families and administrators always have the real picture.
