What is a campus management system?

A campus management system runs the academic and administrative life of a campus: enrolment, classes and sections, daily attendance, assessments, fee collection, staff and payroll, and parent communication. What separates it from a single-school system is scale: it has to keep several campuses straight at once.

Evolvora Campus is structured for exactly that. Each campus keeps its own sections, staff directory and fee ledger, so a teacher only sees their own classes and a branch head only sees their branch, while head office gets one consolidated view across every campus. For a single-site school, our school management system page is the better starting point.
