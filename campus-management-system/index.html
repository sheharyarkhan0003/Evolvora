What is a campus management system?

A campus management system is a single platform for running the academic and administrative life of a campus: enrolment, classes and sections, daily attendance, assessments, fee collection, staff and payroll, and communication with parents.

Evolvora Campus gives admins, teachers and parents their own tailored view of the same live information. Set up your campus structure once and everything, from a teacher marking attendance to a parent checking fees, stays perfectly in sync.
