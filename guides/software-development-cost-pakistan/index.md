# Software development cost in Pakistan (2026)

By [Sheharyar Khan](https://evolvoratech.com/about/), founder of Evolvora Technologies · Published 11 October 2026 · Updated 11 October 2026

In 2026, **software development in Pakistan** typically costs Rs 150,000 to 600,000 for a business website, Rs 1.5 to 5 million for the first version of a custom web application, Rs 2 to 6 million for a mobile app on Android and iOS, and Rs 4 million or more for a SaaS product. The biggest price drivers are the number of user roles, the integrations and who maintains the software afterwards.

In short

-   Price follows **scope**: user roles, screens and integrations, more than technology choice.
-   Budget another **15 to 20 percent a year** for maintenance, plus hosting and messaging.
-   Payments, SMS and WhatsApp each add work **and** a running cost.
-   Get a quote only after a short **discovery phase**, and compare quotes on the same written scope.

## What drives the price?

### 1\. User roles and screens

Every type of user (customer, staff, manager, admin) needs its own screens and permissions. A system with one role is far cheaper than one with four. Evolvora Campus, for example, has separate views for admins, teachers and parents, and each one is a product in its own right.

### 2\. Integrations

Connecting to other systems is where estimates most often slip: [JazzCash and EasyPaisa payments](https://evolvoratech.com/guides/jazzcash-easypaisa-integration/), bank transfers, SMS and WhatsApp messaging, accounting software or an existing database. Each integration needs its own testing and error handling.

### 3\. Platforms

A web app that works well on phones is the cheapest way to reach most users. Separate Android and iOS apps cost more, although a cross-platform framework keeps that to one codebase. Decide early which you really need.

### 4\. Design depth

A simple, clean interface built from a design system costs far less than custom illustrations and animation. For most business software, clarity matters more than decoration.

### 5\. Data migration

Moving records from spreadsheets or an old system takes real effort, especially when the data is messy. Ask whether it is included in the quote.

### 6\. Who maintains it afterwards

Software needs updates, security fixes and small changes for as long as you use it. A quote that ends at launch is only part of the cost.

## Freelancer, software company or in-house team?

The same project can be priced very differently depending on who builds it.

-   **Freelancer:** the lowest price, best for small, well-defined jobs. The risk is continuity: if they become busy or unavailable, the knowledge leaves with them.
-   **Software company:** costs more, because the price includes design, testing, project management and a team that stays after launch.
-   **In-house team:** makes sense once software is core to your business and there is enough work to keep developers busy all year. Developer salaries in Pakistan are rising quickly; see [Emerhub's salary overview](https://emerhub.com/pakistan/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-software-developers-in-pakistan/) for current ranges, then add recruitment, equipment and management time.

## Running costs most quotes leave out

These costs continue every month or year after launch. Ask for them in writing with the quote.

-   **Hosting and cloud services:** from a few thousand rupees a month for a small app to much more for heavy traffic or large files.
-   **App store accounts:** Google Play charges a one-time USD 25 registration fee; Apple's developer programme costs USD 99 a year.
-   **Messages:** SMS and WhatsApp Business messages are charged per message, which adds up quickly for alerts and reminders.
-   **Payment fees:** wallet, card and bank payments carry a fee per transaction, set by the provider.
-   **Maintenance:** a common rule of thumb is 15 to 20 percent of the build cost each year, or a monthly support retainer.
-   **Domain, email and third-party services:** maps, email delivery, analytics and similar services often have monthly plans.

## Fixed price or time and materials?

**Fixed price** works when the scope is clear and written down, which is why it should follow a discovery phase. It protects your budget, but changes cost extra. **Time and materials** (paying for the hours worked) suits ongoing work where priorities change week to week. A common and fair arrangement is a fixed price for the first version, then a monthly retainer for improvements.

## How to get an accurate quote

1.  **Write a one-page brief:** the problem, who will use the software, and what success looks like.
2.  **List the user roles and the main tasks** each one needs to do.
3.  **List every integration:** payments, messaging, existing systems, data to import.
4.  **Ask for a short discovery phase** before a fixed price. A firm that quotes a complex system without asking questions is guessing.
5.  **Compare like for like:** make sure every quote covers design, testing, deployment, data migration and support, and that the code and accounts will be in your name.
