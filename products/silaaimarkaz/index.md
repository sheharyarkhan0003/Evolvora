## What is SilaaiMarkaz?

**SilaaiMarkaz** (also written Silaai Markaz or Silai Markaz) is a tailoring marketplace: a place where people who want clothes stitched meet the tailors, or Darzis, who stitch them. If you are looking for a tailor in Lahore, for ladies suits, bridal wear, men's shalwar kameez or simple alterations, it lets you compare shops before you visit one. It fixes the parts of getting clothes made that usually go wrong, like the price changing at the shop, measurements taken again every time, and designs lost in a chat thread.

SilaaiMarkaz is built and run by [Evolvora Technologies](https://evolvoratech.com/about/), the Lahore software house behind [Evolvora Campus](https://evolvoratech.com/products/evolvora-campus/). It works on Android and on the web at [silaaimarkaz.com](https://silaaimarkaz.com/), in English and Urdu.
