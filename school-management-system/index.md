## What is a school management system?

A **school management system** is software that replaces the scattered registers, spreadsheets and message groups a school uses day to day. Instead of tracking attendance in one place, fees in another and results somewhere else, everything lives in one connected system that admins, teachers and parents can all access.

Evolvora Campus was built for real schools. Set it up once (your classes, sections, staff and students) and information flows automatically. Teachers record attendance and marks from the classroom, the office tracks fees and salaries, and parents are notified instantly on their phones.
